Our Work

Empowering Lives Through Compassionate Care

Since 2003, we have been creating positive change across Kerala through dedicated programs designed to uplift communities and transform lives.

Medical Support

We provide comprehensive medical assistance to those suffering from chronic illnesses and accident victims through hospital transportation, financial aid for treatments, and ongoing care.

Emergency Transport
Medicine Support
Treatment Funds

Rehabilitation

For those left helpless by circumstance, we provide comprehensive rehabilitation services including shelter, skills training, emotional support, and pathways to sustainable livelihoods.

Secure Housing
Counseling
Job Training

Educational Assistance

We believe education is the pathway to self-reliance. Our program adopts underprivileged students, providing financial aid, academic guidance, mentorship, and resources for their educational journey.

School Materials
Tutoring
Scholarships

Punnyam Bala Bhavanam
