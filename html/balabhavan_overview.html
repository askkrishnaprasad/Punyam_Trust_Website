About Us

A Home for Every Child

Punnyam Bala Bhavan is more than just an orphanage - it's a vibrant community where children find family, purpose, and possibility.

Established in 2003, our institution has grown from humble beginnings to become a beacon of hope for orphaned and underprivileged boys in Kerala. We provide not just shelter, but a comprehensive environment for holistic development.

Our approach balances academic excellence with emotional support, cultural enrichment, and spiritual guidance, helping each child achieve their full potential and become self-reliant members of society.

Our Journey

History & Milestones

The story of Punnyam Bala Bhavan is one of perseverance, growth, and unwavering commitment to children's welfare.

2003

Foundation

Punnyam Charitable Trust established Bala Bhavan with just 5 children in a small rented building.

2007

First Building

Construction of our first dedicated building, allowing us to expand our capacity to 15 children.

2010

Government Recognition

Received official recognition from the Kerala State Government, validating our commitment to excellent care standards.

2015

Current Facility

Moved to our current larger facility with enhanced amenities, expanding our capacity to 30 children.

Today

Looking Forward

Continuing to grow and improve, with plans for expanded educational programs and facilities upgrades.

Mission & Values

Compassion

We approach each child with deep empathy and loving care, recognizing their unique struggles and needs.

Education

We believe quality education is the foundation for self-reliance and future success.

Community

Our Facilities

We've created a supportive and nurturing environment designed to meet the needs of our children.

Dormitories

Comfortable, well-maintained sleeping areas arranged by age groups, designed to feel like home.

Study Rooms

Quiet, well-lit spaces equipped with learning materials and resources for academic success.

Dining Hall

A communal space where nutritious meals are served and children gather as a family.

Computer Lab

Modern technology facilities to develop digital literacy and technical skills.

Play Area

Safe outdoor spaces for sports, games, and physical activities essential for healthy development.

Prayer Hall

A serene space dedicated to spiritual practices and reflection.

Daily Life

Activities & Programs

We offer a rich variety of activities that promote holistic development and life skills.

Academic Support

Daily supervised study sessions, homework assistance, and tutoring to ensure academic excellence.

Arts & Culture

Classes in traditional music, dance, and arts to preserve cultural heritage and develop creative expression.

Regular sports, yoga, and outdoor activities to promote physical fitness and teamwork.

Daily prayer, meditation, and values education to foster spiritual growth and moral development.

Practical skills development including cooking, cleaning, and basic financial literacy.
