Fr. Thomas Vadakkel: The Visionary Behind Punnyam Trust

For over two decades, Fr. Thomas has dedicated his life to serving the underprivileged in Kerala. His journey began with a simple vision: to create a haven for orphaned children that would provide not just shelter, but love, education, and opportunities.

Under his leadership, Punnyam Trust has expanded from a small initiative to a comprehensive charitable organization that has touched thousands of lives through its various programs.
